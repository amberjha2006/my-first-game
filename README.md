# 🎮 CASE: THE HOLLOW VILLAGE

> 10 suspects. 10 secrets. 1 truth.

A 3D AI-powered social detective game built for AGENTHON 2026.

## About

Investigate Hollow Village after the ancient artifact known as The Hollow Eye disappears from a supposedly secure shrine.

Explore the village, investigate physical clues, interrogate NPCs using AI-powered dialogue, discover contradictions, and determine who is responsible.

## Features

- 3D first-person exploration
- AI-powered NPC conversations
- 10 unique NPCs
- NPC personality, knowledge, memory, trust and suspicion systems
- Physical evidence and clues
- Multiple investigation cases
- Detective/social deduction gameplay

## Technology

- Godot 4.7.2
- GDScript
- Python
- FastAPI
- FreeLLMAPI
